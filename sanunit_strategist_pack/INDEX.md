## Roster shape (quick orientation)
Three factions — EDA, Chosen, Guard — each with the same structural slots:
air (bomber/AA fighter/scout/gunship/transport/drone carrier), land (tank/
raider/artillery/engineer/scout/sniper/T4 experimentals), naval (frigate,
+Chosen has a T3 battleship), and structures (defence, economy, factories,
tech centres, intel, shield). Only a minority of the ~283 templates are
currently playable — most naval and many T2+ support units are `NO_MODEL` or
`OK_PENDING_APPROVAL`. Always check `UNITS_STATUS.md`.
